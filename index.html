Valerie V2

Valerie V2 is a personal assistant that runs on its developer's own computer. It is used by one person, its developer, and is not offered to the public.

The app connects to the developer's own Gmail account to search and read messages, mark them read or unread, star, archive and label them, move them to Trash, and create drafts for the developer to review and send manually. It cannot send email.
